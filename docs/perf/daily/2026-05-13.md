### [🟢] Unnecessary requestAnimationFrame opacity toggling

- **Root cause**: The `forceRerasterize` function attempts to manually force GPU re-rasterization by briefly setting `wrapper.style.opacity` to `0.9999` and back to `1` over multiple `requestAnimationFrame` ticks. - **Expected gain**: Eliminates paint and layout spikes when cards settle into place, relying on the browser's native layer compositor updates.
