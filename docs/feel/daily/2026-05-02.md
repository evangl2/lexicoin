### [🟡] Camera Center transition is abrupt and stiff
- **What happens now**: Clicking the center button yanks the camera to the middle with a very tight spring, settling almost immediately without any sense of momentum.
- **Why it feels off**: Easing is too rigid, lacking follow-through. A camera moving across a large canvas should feel like it has physical mass.
- **Location**: `src/app/hooks/useCanvasCamera.ts:22`
- **Proposed change**: `stiffness: 200, damping: 20 → stiffness: 120, damping: 18`
- **Why this works**: Lower stiffness and slight underdamping (overshoot) gives the camera "weight" and makes the arrival feel like a physical landing rather than a mechanical snap.
- **Confidence**: High

### [🟡] FlyTo navigation feels robotic
- **What happens now**: When flying to a target, the camera uses a critically damped spring, meaning it decelerates perfectly into the target with zero overshoot.
- **Why it feels off**: Perfect critical damping (easing) feels mechanical and robotic, lacking the organic follow-through expected of a physical lens moving into place.
- **Location**: `src/app/hooks/useCanvasCamera.ts:28`
- **Proposed change**: `stiffness: 100, damping: 20 → stiffness: 85, damping: 14`
- **Why this works**: Introducing a small amount of overshoot (underdamping) confirms the camera has "arrived" by settling into the target, adding organic game feel.
- **Confidence**: High

### [🟡] Zoom inertia drops off too harshly
- **What happens now**: When zooming with a trackpad, the momentum stops very quickly, feeling like the camera is moving through thick mud.
- **Why it feels off**: Easing/Follow-through is cut short. The friction is too high, preventing a satisfying glide after releasing the fingers.
- **Location**: `src/config/physics.ts:9` (`ZOOM_FRICTION`)
- **Proposed change**: `ZOOM_FRICTION: 0.72 → 0.85`
- **Why this works**: A longer follow-through tail on continuous zoom feels more responsive and matches standard OS-level kinetic scrolling expectations.
- **Confidence**: Medium
