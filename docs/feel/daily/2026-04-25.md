### [🟡] Camera Centering lacks anticipation and settles too mechanically
- **What happens now**: Clicking the center button makes the camera translate smoothly to the center, but the `type: "spring", stiffness: 200, damping: 20` configuration produces a linear-feeling, robotic glide with no wind-up or overshoot.
- **Why it feels off**: The action is purely functional (mechanical/robotic intent) but Canvas interactions are organic. It lacks overshoot which would add weight to the camera movement.
- **Location**: `src/app/hooks/useCanvasCamera.ts:22`
- **Proposed change**:
  - `stiffness: 200, damping: 20` → `stiffness: 150, damping: 15, mass: 1.2`
- **Why this works**: A slightly lower damping ratio and higher mass introduces a gentle overshoot, confirming the camera has settled to its new anchor point.
- **Confidence**: High

### [🟡] Camera FlyTo lacks weight
- **What happens now**: Flying to a specific target point (`targetX`, `targetY`) uses a `stiffness: 100, damping: 20` spring. This is heavily overdamped, resulting in a sluggish slide that slowly crawls to a halt.
- **Why it feels off**: It violates the easing and follow-through principles. The high damping kills any momentum, making the camera feel sluggish.
- **Location**: `src/app/hooks/useCanvasCamera.ts:28`
- **Proposed change**:
  - `stiffness: 100, damping: 20` → `stiffness: 120, damping: 18, mass: 1`
- **Why this works**: Increasing stiffness slightly and lowering damping gives the camera a bit more pep while flying, and a softer settle.
- **Confidence**: Medium

### [🟢] Pan limits are strict walls
- **What happens now**: Panning into the edge of the world canvas stops immediately without any spring or bounce when dragging.
- **Why it feels off**: Lack of squash/stretch or elastic deformation on impact. The hard wall feels digital.
- **Location**: `src/app/components/ui/canvas/Canvas.tsx:clampCamera`
- **Proposed change**: Allow `useGesture` to over-pan slightly on the axis and spring back when the mouse is released, rather than hard-clamping inside `onDrag`.
- **Why this works**: Elastic boundaries make the world feel tangible rather than like a digital clipping plane.
- **Confidence**: High
